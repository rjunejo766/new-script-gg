## 🎮 1. Jump to Steal Brainrots

### ⚡ Loadstring (Execute in Roblox)
```lua
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rjunejo766/new-script-gg/main/JumpToStealBrainrots.lua?t=" .. tostring(os.time())))()
```

### ✨ Features (Exact 4 Features)
1. 💰 **Auto Collect Cash**: Automatically collects all cash/income ($836, etc.) and pads generated by your base.
2. 🏃 **WalkSpeed Boost (50)**: Move around the map with high speed.
3. 🕊️ **Fly Mode**: Smooth flight in camera direction with WASD & Space.
4. 🦘 **Infinite Jump**: Unlimited air jumping.
