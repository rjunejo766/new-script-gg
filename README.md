## 🎮 1. Jump to Steal Brainrots

### ⚡ Loadstring (Execute in Roblox)
```lua
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rjunejo766/new-script-gg/main/JumpToStealBrainrots.lua"))()
```

### ✨ Features (Exact 5 Features)
1. 🗡️ **Auto Equip & Teleport**: Automatically equips weapon and teleports directly onto Brainrots across the field.
2. 💰 **Auto Collect Cash**: Automatically collects all cash/income ($836, etc.) generated by your base.
3. 🏃 **WalkSpeed Boost (50)**: Move around the map with high speed.
4. 🕊️ **Fly Mode**: Smooth flight in camera direction with WASD & Space.
5. 🦘 **Infinite Jump**: Unlimited air jumping.
