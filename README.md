## 🎮 1. Jump to Steal Brainrots

### ⚡ Loadstring (Execute in Roblox)
```lua
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rjunejo766/new-script-gg/main/JumpToStealBrainrots.lua"))()
```

### ✨ Features
- 🧠 **Auto Steal Brainrots**: Teleports to Brainrots across the map, grabs them, and deposits them at your base.
- 💰 **Auto Collect Cash**: Automatically collects all cash/income generated by your base droppers.
- 🔄 **Auto Rebirth**: Automatically performs rebirth when requirements are met for permanent multipliers.
- ⚡ **Auto Upgrade Jump & Speed**: Automatically purchases Jump Power and WalkSpeed upgrades.
- 🏃 **WalkSpeed Boost (50)**: Move around the map with high speed.
- 🦘 **Infinite Jump**: Unlimited air jumping.
